Fader

ProductionStudio (frame id, blank = a capital)TrunksCallers / minHold (s)On air from (local h)to

A production makes one studio available with that many trunks and aims that many callers a minute at it from everywhere else while it is on air (studio local time, from its longitude; -1 = all day). When every trunk made available (or every user port) at the studio is taken the caller hears busy: a denial that never reaches the router. The fader lives in TRUNK-SIMULATOR/fader.json, is re-read every tick (editing the file works too) and is always within sane bounds. Speed 0 freezes the simulated clock; real requests are always served. Every site follows its own local time, so the television and radio surges sweep around the globe; the Olympics happen once a day at one UTC moment, so every site surges together and callers from everywhere aim at the venue. Every building with two or more frames also makes its own intercom calls between them at a flat rate. Scrub the day in the footer of any page.
