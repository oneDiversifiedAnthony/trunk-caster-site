Trunk-Caster speaks by protocol, not by brand. Every protocol found in the manuals is advertised as 10 frames of 56+8 ports; a caster is a trunk port and a trunk between two casters carries 8 channels. Any frame can reach any other frame directly, or via another frame when the direct capacity runs out. Every crosspoint is expressed in the Ward-Beck line SITE,FRAME,INPUT,INPUT_NAME,CLOSE/OPEN,OUTPUT_NAME,OUTPUT,FRAME,SITE.

Intercom simulators and remote frames

Each intercom simulator is its own container hosting one protocol's frames. It connects to the Ward-Beck port 0.0.0.0:7879, announces its frames, receives every CLOSE/OPEN line for them, echoes each one back as its acknowledgement, and checks in periodically. Start them with docker compose --profile sims up -d.
